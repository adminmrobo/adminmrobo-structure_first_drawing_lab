## f1) fail_f1_haru_lost_buns — 캐릭터 고정값 누락
alt: 분홍 머리 소녀가 그려졌지만 하루의 상징인 정수리 번 두 개와 앞머리 가운데 남색 줄이 사라져 다른 사람처럼 보이는 실패 예시
```
Intentional mistake example for a drawing textbook. Full body anime illustration, vertical 2:3, plain off-white background.
A young adult woman in a lavender hoodie, open white lab coat, rolled-up jeans and lavender sneakers, standing three-quarter view, right hand on hip, light from the upper left — BUT her hair is plain long straight pink hair with NO hair buns, NO navy streaks, NO ahoge, and her eyes are green instead of blue.
Draw it cleanly and confidently, the mistake must be clearly visible.
```

## f2) fail_f2_moa_mirrored — 좌우 반전
alt: 모아 그림이 좌우로 뒤집혀 왼쪽 귀 위에 있어야 할 흰 리본이 오른쪽에 있고, 손도 반대쪽 허리에 있는 실패 예시
```
Intentional mistake example for a drawing textbook. Full body anime illustration, vertical 2:3, plain off-white background.
Moa from the attached illustration, but the whole image is horizontally MIRRORED: she is turned slightly to the LEFT, her LEFT hand is on her hip, the white ribbon on her frilled headband is over her RIGHT ear, and the light comes from the upper RIGHT.
Everything else the same: long ash-beige wavy hair, amber eyes, plaid bow tie, camel cardigan, white frilled apron, black skirt, brown loafers.
No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no revealing clothing.
```

## f3) fail_f3_sia_light_conflict — 광원 모순
alt: 시아의 얼굴과 몸은 왼쪽에서 빛을 받는데 바닥 그림자도 왼쪽으로 뻗고 머리 하이라이트는 오른쪽에 있어 빛의 방향이 앞뒤가 맞지 않는 실패 예시
```
Intentional mistake example for a drawing textbook about light. Full body anime illustration, vertical 2:3, plain off-white background.
Sia from the attached illustration (long straight black hair, black maid dress, large gray bow, long white apron, no headdress), standing three-quarter view, right hand on hip — BUT the lighting is inconsistent on purpose: the face and body are brightly lit from the LEFT with shadows on the right side, while the cast shadow on the floor ALSO falls to the LEFT toward the light, and the shiny hair highlight is on the RIGHT side of her head.
The contradiction must be clearly visible.
No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no revealing clothing.
```

## f4) fail_f4_moa_floating_folds — 접점·중력 무시
alt: 바람도 없는데 모아의 치마와 앞치마가 위로 떠 있고, 주름이 몸과 상관없는 곳에서 무늬처럼 퍼져 있는 실패 예시
```
Intentional mistake example for a drawing textbook about fabric folds. Full body anime illustration, vertical 2:3, plain off-white background, completely still air.
Moa from the attached illustration (ash-beige wavy hair, white frilled headband with a ribbon over the left ear, camel cardigan, plaid bow tie, white frilled apron, black knee-length skirt), standing still three-quarter view, right hand on hip — BUT the fabric ignores gravity and body contact: the knee-length skirt and apron float stiffly upward and outward as if frozen, the hair hangs normally, the cardigan sleeves are perfectly straight tubes with no folds at the bent elbow, and random decorative fold lines radiate from the middle of the chest where nothing is pulling.
The skirt stays knee length and modest; the mistake is only the floating shape and wrong fold placement.
No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no revealing clothing.
```
